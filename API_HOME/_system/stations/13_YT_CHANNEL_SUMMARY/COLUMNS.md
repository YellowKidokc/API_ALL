# The spreadsheet columns, one per line:  column name | what DeepSeek should put in the cell
# David's youtube_specific_probes (CrossVideoSynthesis spec v2). Every cell: 20 words or fewer, with a [mm:ss]
# where it points at a moment; "—" when there is nothing notable (the default); "n/a" when the video gives nothing to judge.
# summary (3 sentences) and keywords (2-3) are always there. Channel-level items (shifting_claims across videos,
# hostility_score, prediction ledger, drift, escalation) are not per-video cells: they belong to the channel profile.
speaker | who is speaking: name and role
hermeneutic_method | the interpretive rulebook the speaker uses, and whether they switch rules mid-argument
doctrine_first_appearance | when this reading first appeared historically; any pre-modern witnesses for it (mark "verify")
christ_load_bearing | Christ-removal test: remove Christ from the argument; does it still stand (yes / partly / no) and why
fruit_vector | Law 4 fruits evident, as +/0/- for love joy peace patience kindness goodness faithfulness gentleness self-control; name any anti-fruit
warrant_type_match | the warrant type (scripture, history, science, experience, authority) vs the claim type; matched or mismatched
tier_intensity_gap | doctrinal tier of the main claim (core / secondary / speculative) vs how intensely it is argued; the gap
opponent_fidelity | would the opponents recognize their own view as presented (steelman / fair / strawman), with the example
operative_authority | where the speaker actually goes when sources conflict (scripture, tradition, science, experience, self)
adversary_model | who the enemy is in this video, and whether the enemy explains more than it should (overreach)
one_year_test | what a listener who lived this for one year would look like; the lived consequence
audience_response | from the comments: the strongest pushback and whether the speaker's case survives it ("no comments acquired" if none)
unexplained_correctness | where the speaker is right in a way David's framework cannot yet account for
canon_heatmap | which parts of the canon are cited most and which are conspicuously absent
cost_of_saying | what saying this costs the speaker (reputation, audience, position), or "costless"
load_bearing_unasked_question | the question the whole argument depends on but never asks
avoided_question | a question the speaker raises or is asked and steers around
law_resonance | which of the Ten Laws this resonates with (1 Gravity sin/grace, 2 Motion, 3 EM truth, 4 Strong love, 5 Thermo judgment, 6 Information logos, 7 Quantum faith, 8 Relativity grace, 9 Weak moral conservation, 10 Coherence Christ) and how
pre_theoretical_noticing | something the speaker notices without a theory for it that the framework could explain

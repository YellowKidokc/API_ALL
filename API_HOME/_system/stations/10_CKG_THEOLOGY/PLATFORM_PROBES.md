# Platform layer: YouTube / TikTok probes. NOTE-only: they describe the medium and never spend a FLAG slot.
speaker_incentives | What does the speaker gain (money, audience, status, movement)?
authority_claims_verifiable | Are the credentials or authorities claimed checkable?
source_citations | Are sources named precisely enough to look up?
emotional_register | Calm, urgent, fearful, mocking, reverent?
rhetorical_devices | Which devices carry the weight (repetition, rhetorical questions, us-vs-them)?
thumbnail_title_gap | Does the title promise more than the video delivers?
editing_music_role | Do edits, music or effects do argumentative work?
publication_context | When and why was it published (news event, series, reply video)?
originality_status | Original argument, restated argument, or reaction to another creator?
silent_omissions | What would a fair treatment mention that this leaves out?
shifting_claims | Does a claim change strength or meaning during the video?

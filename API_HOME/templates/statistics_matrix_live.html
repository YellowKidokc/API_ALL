How to read it in one minute

1. Look at colour first.A band that is mostly blue is a strength. A band with a red left end has a few weak spots; a band that is red most of the way needs work.
2. Then look at size.Big circles are the statistics furthest from normal, good or bad. Those are the ones worth reading.
3. Flip the comparison.Switch between your corpus and academic norms. A family that turns red only under academic norms is your house style, not a flaw in this paper.
4. Check the inner shape.Diamonds were judged by the AI, dots were computed. A ring means the two AI runs disagreed, so treat that number with care.
5. Use the family map for the verdict.Top right is strong on both counts. Top left is strong for you but weak academically. The dashed lane holds families that have no academic benchmark at all.
